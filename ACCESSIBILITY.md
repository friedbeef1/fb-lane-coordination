# Flow Builder accessibility

Flow Builder presents its operating guidance as Markdown, plain-language
summaries, descriptive links and tables. Critical instructions and delivery
states must be available as text rather than conveyed by color, images or
diagrams alone. Diagrams supplement the written workflow.

The plugin runs inside Codex. Its support for keyboard navigation, screen
readers, zoom and other assistive technologies depends on the Codex client.
Flow Builder does not claim a formal accessibility certification or completed
assistive-technology audit. Consumer applications need their own accessibility
requirements and verification.

Report an accessibility problem at
[GitHub issues](https://github.com/friedbeef1/fb-lane-coordination/issues),
including the affected document or step and the relevant client/assistive
technology. Keep private project data out of public reports. Maintainers will
review reproducible reports and offer a text-based alternative where practical.
